Common questions

What production rate should I use?

Use your own tracked rate: total labor hours divided by square feet on similar buildings you already clean. Published rates vary widely by building type, so this calculator does not guess one for you.

What is the difference between break-even and target price?

Break-even covers direct cost and overhead, so profit is zero. Target price adds the net margin you need. Bid at or above target; treat break-even as your walk-away floor.

Why model extra hours?

Contracts usually do not fail on day one. Hours creep in. The what-if rows show how little cushion a price has.
