Privacy

The calculators

Numbers you type into a calculator stay in your browser. They are not sent to us.

Page statistics

We count page views, whether someone changed a calculator field, and clicks on the kit and workbook links. We store the site section visited, the referring website's name, and a one-way hash of your IP address and browser that is combined with a secret that changes daily, so it cannot be reversed or linked across days. We store no cookies and no IP addresses. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we send nothing.

Email sign-up

If you ask for the Contract Rescue Kit we store your email address, the page you signed up on, the date, and the version of the consent text. We use it to send the kit and occasional pricing tips and we will not sell or share it. Every email will include an unsubscribe option.

Feedback

Messages you send through the feedback box are stored with the page name and date. Please do not include personal details.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare (database and Worker). The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

Use the feedback box on any page, write REMOVE and the email address you used, and we will delete it. A dedicated contact address will be added here once one exists.

Estimates only. Not accounting, legal or tax advice. Starting numbers on every calculator are examples, not market data.
