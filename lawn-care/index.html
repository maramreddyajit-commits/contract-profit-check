Common questions

How do I handle seasons?

Run the calculator for the busy-season visit rate and again for the slow season, then compare against what you charge.

What belongs in equipment?

Blade and trimmer line wear, repairs, mower depreciation and your share of the truck and trailer.

Why include travel per visit?

Drive time and fuel to each stop can eat the margin on small properties.
