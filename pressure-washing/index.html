Common questions

Why add extra hours?

Setup, hose runs, protecting plants, rinsing and cleanup are real labor that square-foot rates leave out.

What about surface type?

Use a production rate for the surface you are washing. Concrete, wood and siding clean at different speeds; track your own.

Should I price by area or by hour?

Whichever you use, run the job here to see if it covers cost and margin. A flat price that ignores hours is the usual way jobs lose money.
